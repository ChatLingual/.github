# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities **privately**. Do not open a public issue or pull request.

- Email **security@cxconnect.ai** with "SECURITY" in the subject line, or
- Use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on the repository's Security tab, if it is enabled.

Please include:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The affected repository, version or commit, if known
- Your contact details, if you would like updates

## What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- We will keep you updated as we investigate and fix the issue.
- We ask that you give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

## Scope

This policy covers repositories in the ChatLingual GitHub organization and the ChatLingual products and services they support. Please do not test against other customers' data or degrade the service, and report only what you find.
